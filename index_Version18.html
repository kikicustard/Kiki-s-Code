🏓 Purple Pong

Ready to rally?

📜 Rules

  • First player to score 5 points wins the round.
  • The ball bounces off paddles and the top and bottom walls.
  • If the ball passes a paddle, the other player scores.
  • The ball serves again automatically after every point.
  • Press Next Round after a round ends.
  • Another ball joins every 3 levels.
  • Points and levels unlock paddle colors and ball characters.

🎮 How to play

  • Player 1 moves with W / S or ↑ / ↓.
  • In 2-player mode, Player 2 uses ↑ / ↓.
  • In solo mode, the CPU uses medium difficulty.
  • Pause with Space, the Pause button, or a game-area click.
